The journey so far

A short history of how OMOP has taken root in Sweden.

  1. 2018

    The first early adopters

    AstraZeneca and WHO's Uppsala Monitoring Center begin exploring OMOP and join the consortium behind EHDEN, an Innovative Medicines Initiative project supported by the EU's Horizon 2020 programme and EFPIA.

  2. The years that followed

    Swedish data partners

    Several organisations become data partners with EHDEN Foundation and/or EMA's DARWIN EU, including:

    • Karolinska Institutet – Department of Medical Epidemiology and Biostatistics (MEB)
    • Linköping University – Department of Biomedical and Clinical Sciences (BKV), which mapped the Swedish Inflammatory Bowel Disease Registry (SWIBREG) to OMOP
    • University of Gothenburg – the SCIFI-PEARL research project

  3. April 2025

    First Swedish partner in DARWIN EU

    SCIFI-PEARL becomes the first Swedish data partner in EMA's DARWIN EU network.

  4. March 2026

    Government assignment

    The Government tasks the National Board of Health and Welfare with investigating the prerequisites, costs and benefits of making Swedish health registries available according to the OMOP CDM.

  5. Today

    A growing community

    More organisations are exploring the potential of OMOP and the methods and tools from the open-science network OHDSI.

OMOP users in Sweden

Organisations and initiatives in Sweden working with the OMOP Common Data Model.

The Government Offices

Assignment to investigate OMOP adaptation of Swedish health registries

Government assignment to investigate and describe prerequisites, costs, and benefits of making Swedish health registries available according to OMOP CDM to facilitate international collaboration and research.

Visit website →

Karolinska Institutet - MEB

Implemented OMOP for studies on older adults and healthcare use

Mapped Swedish registry data to OMOP CDM via EHDEN and became a data partner to EHDEN Foundation. Also used OMOP CDM in Stockholm CREAtinine Measurements to quantify medication errors and CKD burden.

Visit website →

University of Gothenburg, SCIFI-PEARL

Largest and most active Swedish OMOP implementation

National linked observational study with regular updates. Data partner to EHDEN Foundation and, since April 2025, to EMA's DARWIN-EU.

Visit website →

SWIBREG

National quality registry for inflammatory bowel disease

Mapped to OMOP through EHDEN and data partner to EHDEN Foundation.

Visit website →

Uppsala Monitoring Center

Early OMOP adopter for pharmacovigilance signal management

Leader in using Real-World Evidence and federated OMOP-harmonized datasets in pharmacovigilance studies.

Visit website →

Lund University, Intelligent Systems Laboratory

AI-based decision support for colon cancer care

Contributes to FLORENCE with expertise in trustworthy and robust AI and privacy-preserving federated learning on OMOP data.

Visit website →

The Swedish Medical Products Agency

Swedish DARWIN EU node since February 2025

DARWIN EU is established by EMA and the European Medicines Regulatory Network to provide timely and reliable evidence on medicine use, safety, and effectiveness from EU healthcare databases.
