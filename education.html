Healthcare Data Standards

Learn how key healthcare data standards enable interoperable digital health systems, explore the differences between openEHR and FHIR, and understand how they can work together in practice. openEHR. FHIR. IHE. LOINC. ICD-11. OMOP If you work in healthcare, you've seen these everywhere. But knowing the names is not the same as understanding what problem each standard solves, how they differ, and how they work together in practice. That is why the Healthcare data standards course was launched on Hive.
